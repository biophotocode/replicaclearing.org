Replication of Endogenous Patterns via Label Incorporation in Chemoablated Acrylamide (REPLICA)

Welcome! This website describes replication of endogenous patterns via label incorporation in chemoablated acrylamide (REPLICA), a new approach to tissue clearing developed at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Cambridge, UK, and described in an upcoming manuscript.
